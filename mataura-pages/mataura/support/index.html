Common questions

The river flow isn't updating / shows an old reading. Flow and water temperature come live from Environment Southland. If your phone is offline, the app shows the last reading it successfully fetched, marked as such. Pull down to refresh once you have signal. Readings are indicative only — always assess conditions yourself before heading out.

The river camera isn't showing for my reach. Cameras are provided by Environment Southland and currently exist for a limited number of sites. More are planned for the Mataura catchment — they'll appear automatically as they come online.

What are the bag limits and season for my reach? Each reach shows its own rules — bag limit, methods and season — on the River screen, summarised from the Fish & Game Southland Anglers Notice. The official notice is the only legal authority; always confirm before you fish.

Whitebaiting rules? The Reference tab has a Whitebaiting section with the season, hours and rules for the lower Mataura, summarised from the Department of Conservation. DOC's regulations are the legal authority.

Access to the river. A fishing licence doesn't grant access across private land. Always ask the landowner, respect the Upper Mataura beat system, and use the official maps linked in the app.

Subscriptions

Premium (NZ$59.99/year) and Guide (NZ$249.99/year) are annual subscriptions with a 7-day free trial, billed through your Apple ID and renewing automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Manage or cancel: open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → Mataura River Fishing. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

Restore a purchase: if you've reinstalled the app or changed devices, open the paywall in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Upgrade from Premium to Guide: in the app, go to Settings or the Reference tab and tap Upgrade to Guide. Apple credits your remaining Premium time automatically.

Your data & privacy

The app has no accounts and no tracking. Your catch log, photos and notes stay on your device. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Report a problem or suggest a feature

Email annamareemorris@gmail.com with your device and iOS version, what you expected and what happened, and a screenshot if you can. Feature ideas are genuinely welcome — this app is built with and for Southland anglers.

Privacy Policy — Mataura River Fishing

Effective date: 3 July 2026  ·  Developer: NZ Heritage Nature (Anna-Maree Morris)  ·  Contact: annamareemorris@gmail.com

Mataura River Fishing (“the app”) is a fly-fishing companion for the Mataura River and Southland. We've built it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what that means in plain terms.

The short version

  • We don't have accounts, and we don't ask you to log in.
  • We don't track you, show ads, or use any analytics or advertising services.
  • We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information.
  • Anything you record in the app stays on your device.

Information you enter stays on your device

Your catch log, photos, notes, guide client details, and reach notes are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device database. We have no server, and we never receive or see this information. If you have iCloud device backup switched on, this data may be included in your own private Apple backup — that's between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy terms, and is not accessible to us. You can delete any entry, or remove the app entirely, to erase this data at any time.

We don't use your location

The app shows fixed river-monitoring sites. It does not request or use your device's GPS location.

Information sent when the app loads data

To show live conditions, the app requests information from these sources. As with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to the server it contacts, but the app does not attach any personal identifier to these requests:

  • Environment Southland — river flow, water temperature, and webcam images.
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) — weather and forecast data, covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • A small configuration file hosted by the developer, read on launch. It contains no personal data and sends none.

Links to external sites (such as Fish & Game New Zealand and Herenga ā Nuku) open in your browser and are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you turn on flow alerts, the app schedules reminders on your device. There is no push server, and no information leaves your device to provide them.

Subscriptions

Premium and Guide subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Apple handles all payment information — we never receive or store your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in your App Store account settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the revised version at this address.

Your rights and contact

Because we don't collect your personal information, there's nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side — it's all on your device, under your control. If you have any questions, contact annamareemorris@gmail.com. This policy is provided under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

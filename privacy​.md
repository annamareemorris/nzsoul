# Privacy Policy — The Bannockburn App

**Last updated: 11 May 2026**

The Bannockburn App ("the app") is a community tourism and heritage guide for 
Bannockburn, Central Otago, New Zealand. This policy explains what information 
the app accesses and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.**

The app does not require an account or login. No data you interact with is sent 
to our servers or shared with third parties.

## Location

The app requests access to your device's location when the Explore map is in use. 
Your location is displayed on the map to help you navigate Bannockburn's trails, 
wineries, and heritage sites, and to calculate walking or driving directions.

- Your location is used only on your device
- It is never stored, logged, or transmitted
- Location access can be denied or revoked at any time in iOS Settings without 
  affecting most app features

## Calendar

The app requests write-only access to your calendar when you choose to add an 
arts or community event. The app writes the event you select and nothing more — 
it does not read, browse, or collect any of your existing calendar data.

## Audio

Background audio playback is used in the History tab so that narrated audio 
books continue playing when your screen locks. No audio data is recorded or 
transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

The app displays photos loaded from Wikimedia Commons and iNaturalist. These are 
standard image requests; no personal data is sent with them. The app does not use 
advertising networks, analytics services, or crash-reporting tools.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect any data from any user, 
including children.
